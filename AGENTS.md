# Who Am I Service

Spring Boot 4 service: hexagonal modules with Spring Modulith, Cucumber for every test, Postgres.

- Decisions and their reasons are in `docs/adr/` (index: `docs/adr/README.md`). Read the
  relevant record before changing anything it covers; never load the whole folder.
- Formatting and IDE setup: `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
- Before finishing: `mvn spotless:apply`, then `mvn verify` must pass.
